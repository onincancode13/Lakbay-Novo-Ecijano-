About Lakbay, Novo Ecijano

Discover the beauty, culture, and heritage of Nueva Ecija. Lakbay, Novo Ecijano is your gateway to exploring the province’s rich history, breathtaking landscapes, heirloom flavors, and vibrant communities—from limestone river canyons to tranquil highland dams.
