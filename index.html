About Lakbay, Novo Ecijano

Discover the beauty, culture, and heritage of Nueva Ecija. Lakbay, Novo Ecijano is your gateway to exploring the province’s rich history, breathtaking landscapes, and vibrant communities—from limestone river canyons to tranquil highland dams.

Heart of the Province

Culture & Heritage

Nueva Ecija is known as the Rice Granary of the Philippines, home to traditions celebrating agriculture, community, and resilience.

Vibrant Festivals

From the Taong Putik Festival in Aliaga to the Tanduyong Festival in San Jose, every town celebrates faith and harvest with color.

Local Cuisine

Savor Cabanatuan’s famous garlic longganisa, savory batotay, carabao milk pastillas, and freshly brewed highland coffee.

Craft & Resilience

Discover centuries-old churches, agricultural heritage, and warm Novo Ecijano hospitality passed down through generations.
